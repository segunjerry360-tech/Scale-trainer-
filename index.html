Examples, export and import
Each drill remembers everything on screen when you add it: a two-note loop, a scale in one box, a scale through several keys, a phrase. It also has its own time and its own start and end tempo (the tempo glides, up or down). Tap Use current settings to change what a drill plays. A program replaces the single practice timer while it runs.
Phrases and riffs
Phrases play inside the current box, and follow your box and key settings. While a phrase is on, the sequence and string-skipping choices are not used. The one-note-per-slot length follows the Notes per beat setting.
